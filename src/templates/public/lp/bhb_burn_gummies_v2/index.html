How a 34-year-old nurse from Cleveland brought her blood sugar back to normal — without going on a diet

After a pre-diabetic diagnosis at 34, Claire Hargreaves refused another restrictive diet. Instead, she found a formula built on six natural ingredients that helped balance her blood sugar at its source. Here is her story — in her own words.

By The Health Desk Updated: September 2026  ·  8 min read

Claire Hargreaves remembers exactly when she was told her blood sugar was heading in the wrong direction. It was a Tuesday morning in January 2024, halfway through a twelve-hour shift at MetroHealth Medical Center, when a routine set of bloods came back with her HbA1c firmly in the pre-diabetic range. She had just finished caring for a patient in his fifties — admitted with complications from long-standing type 2 diabetes — and caught herself staring at her results thinking: that could be me in fifteen years.

"I was 34, I was a nurse, and I already knew exactly what that number meant," Claire says. "But every professional I spoke to told me the answer was the same thing I'd already tried a dozen times — another diet. Cut the carbs. Cut the sugar. Cut everything I actually enjoyed eating."

Claire had tried to fix things her doctors' way before — many times. Calorie-counting apps, low-carb diets, a brief and miserable attempt at 5:2 intermittent fasting. Every attempt followed the same pattern: two weeks of genuine effort, one brutal fourteen-hour shift, then a sharp return to the frozen dinners and vending-machine candy that got her through work.

"It isn't willpower," she explains. "It's what happens when your blood sugar is all over the place. You crash, you reach for whatever will lift you back up, and the cycle repeats. No amount of dieting fixes that. In fact, dieting usually makes it worse."

"It isn't willpower. It's what happens when your blood sugar is all over the place. No amount of dieting fixes that."

More than just a number on a chart

Her HbA1c had climbed to 6.1%. Her fasting glucose was raised. She had the classic afternoon crashes — the shaky, foggy exhaustion that hit around 3 p.m. and sent her hunting for a cookie jar. Her energy on shifts was unreliable. Her sleep had deteriorated. And no matter how "well" she tried to eat, the swings kept coming back.

"It wasn't vanity. I could see my health trajectory clearly, and it frightened me. Uncontrolled blood sugar damages nearly every system over time — nerves, kidneys, eyes, the heart. I didn't want to become a patient on my own ward."

Context

Around one in three US adults are estimated to have raised HbA1c levels — the CDC's prediabetes benchmark. Shift work, disrupted sleep and stress are known to worsen blood sugar regulation independently of dietary choices — which is why "just eat less" advice so often fails the people it's aimed at.

The conversation that changed everything

The turning point came through a colleague — a senior nurse called Sophie, who had quietly stopped raiding the cookie jar and, more importantly, no longer looked exhausted at the end of every shift. Claire had noticed but hadn't dared to ask what had changed. Eventually, after that January shift, she brought it up.

"Sophie didn't sell it as a miracle," Claire recalls. "She said she'd stopped trying to diet and started working on the underlying problem instead: her blood sugar. Once that stabilized, everything else fell into place — the cravings, the energy, the weight, the sleep."

Sophie's approach had two parts. The first was a gentle daily movement habit — a thirty-minute walk before her shifts, nothing more ambitious than that. The second was a natural formula she took every morning: Burn Gummies, built around six natural ingredients that dietitians had begun paying serious attention to for their effect on blood sugar regulation.

"She showed me the label. Six ingredients, every single one backed by clinical research on glucose control. Not a stimulant, not a fat burner — a formula designed to help balance blood sugar at its source. That was the phrase she used, and it's the one that finally made sense to me."

Six natural ingredients dietitians were amazed by

The six active ingredients in Burn Gummies have each been researched for their role in blood sugar regulation. Together, the formula is designed to target the problem at its source rather than paper over it with another restrictive diet:

  • Berberine — a plant compound shown in multiple trials to activate AMPK, the enzyme that regulates glucose uptake in cells
  • Chromium — an essential trace mineral that supports insulin sensitivity
  • Cinnamon bark extract — associated with lower post-meal glucose spikes
  • Banaba leaf extract — rich in corosolic acid, studied for its glucose-lowering effect
  • Alpha-lipoic acid — an antioxidant linked to improved insulin function and reduced sugar cravings
  • Gymnema sylvestre — traditionally called "the sugar destroyer," shown to blunt the taste of sweetness and reduce cravings

"None of them are exotic. What matters is the combination — they work on different parts of the same problem," Claire explains. "The dietitians I've spoken to since were genuinely surprised at how well it's put together. That's the whole point of the formula."

Building a routine that can survive real life

Claire was skeptical at first. She had tried single-ingredient supplements before without noticing much. But she ordered a one-month box of Burn Gummies and, more importantly, committed to the walk alongside it.

"What made the difference this time was that I wasn't dieting. I wasn't cutting out bread or pasta. I wasn't giving up the foods I actually enjoy. I was working on the blood sugar itself — and letting my body catch up."

Her routine, as it settled into shape over the first month:

  • One Burn Gummies capsule with a glass of water first thing in the morning, before breakfast
  • A 25- to 30-minute walk before the start of each shift
  • No foods banned — no carb-cutting, no calorie counting, no willpower battles
  • One small habit: pairing protein with the main carbs at every meal to smooth out the glucose curve
  • No food after 9 p.m. — as much for sleep as for anything else

"It sounds modest because it is," she says. "That's the point. I was working with my blood sugar, not against my life."

"Work on your blood sugar, not your willpower."

"The formula helped me most with the cravings. That mid-shift slump where you'd normally reach for something sweet — I stopped feeling it as intensely after the first two weeks. Whether it's the berberine, the gymnema, or the whole six ingredients together, I don't know. But the effect was real."

— Claire Hargreaves, 34, Cleveland

Week by week: what actually happened

Weeks 1–2

Adjustment period. Sugar cravings noticeably quieter. The 3 p.m. crash softened. Sleep started to improve. Fasting glucose down ~10 mg/dL on her home meter. "I felt cleaner, lighter in the mornings. I stopped waking up foggy."

Weeks 3–5

The walks became automatic. Afternoon energy held steady through shifts. First follow-up bloods: fasting glucose already back in the normal range. "I stopped raiding the vending machine during the night. A huge change for me."

Weeks 6–7

Appetite noticeably more controlled. Cravings for sweet food had almost disappeared. Home glucose readings were flat and stable throughout the day. "My colleagues started asking what I'd changed — I hadn't told anyone."

Month 3

Second HbA1c check: 5.5% — back in normal range. Her doctor was visibly surprised at the speed. Blood pressure had also come down. As a side effect, Claire had steadily lost around 30 pounds — without dieting once.

Month 4 (today)

HbA1c holding at 5.4%. Fasting glucose steady. Off the pre-diabetic register. "I feel like myself again. Really myself — with stable energy, no crashes, and food I actually enjoy still on my plate."

What she says made all the difference

Claire is careful not to overstate any single element of her approach. As a healthcare professional, she knows the difference between a genuine physiological change and a quick fix.

"The walk mattered. Being a bit more thoughtful about my meals mattered. But what allowed me to actually keep doing those things consistently was that my blood sugar had finally stopped swinging. When it stops swinging, you stop craving. When you stop craving, everything else becomes possible."

She is also candid about what Burn Gummies is and isn't. "It isn't a magic pill. It isn't a fat burner. It's a natural formula that works on the underlying process. If your blood sugar has been rocky for years, this is the sort of formula that gives your body a proper chance to steady itself — without asking you to give up the food on your plate."
