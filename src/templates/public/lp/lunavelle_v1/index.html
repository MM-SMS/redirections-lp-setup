How it works

Three seconds of effort. Then you are done for the night.

  1. Peel

    Lift one patch off the backing. Thirty seconds before you brush your teeth is the right moment, roughly half an hour before bed.

  2. Press

    Press it onto clean, dry skin. Upper arm, shoulder or inner wrist all work. The adhesive is latex free and holds through the night, including sheets and sleeping on your side.

  3. Sleep

    The botanicals absorb steadily for up to eight hours. Peel the patch off in the morning and throw it away. There is no dose to count and nothing to remember at 3 a.m.

Working with the body, not over it

Your nervous system already has a volume knob.

Calm is not something you have to import. Your brain runs an inhibitory system, built largely around a messenger called GABA, whose entire job is to turn neural activity down as the night comes on. When it works, you get drowsy without deciding to. When it is drowned out by stress, caffeine, shift work and years of poor sleep, you lie there wired and exhausted at the same time.

Lunavelle is formulated around that system rather than against it. Valerian, passionflower and lemon balm are among the oldest botanicals used to support a calm, quiet nervous system, and delivering them through the skin keeps the level even instead of dumping it all at once. It is a nudge in the direction your body is already trying to go, held there all night.
