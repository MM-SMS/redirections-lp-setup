How It Works

Simple Science, Real Results

Froza AC uses evaporative cooling — the same natural principle your body uses when it sweats. Warm air is pulled through a water-saturated filter, where heat energy is absorbed. The air that comes out the other side is measurably cooler and more comfortable.
